Reaction Audio Ducking & Mic Commentary Setup: Broadcast-Grade Audio Guide

By FairReact Audio Engineering Group • Standard OBS Studio & Adobe Premiere Tutorial • Updated September 2026 • 10 Min Read

Clear, balanced audio is the single most important factor distinguishing high-retention reaction channels from amateur uploads. If your microphone voice is muffled beneath loud music or movie sound effects, viewers leave within seconds—and copyright algorithms are more likely to flag uninterrupted background audio.

In this technical guide, we break down professional sidechain compression audio ducking in OBS Studio, broadcast volume target levels (-14 LUFS), and proper multi-track recording configurations.

1. What is Audio Ducking and Why is It Critical?

Audio ducking is an automated dynamics processing technique where the volume of a background audio track (such as the video being reacted to) is automatically reduced whenever a foreground signal (your microphone) exceeds a specific threshold.

Benefits for YouTube reaction channels include:

  • Effortless Intelligibility: Viewers hear every word of your commentary with clarity without having to strain over explosive background sound.
  • Automatic Smoothing: Eliminates the need to manually draw volume keyframes for thousands of reaction pauses in post-production.
  • Fair Use Evidence: Proves to human review teams that the creator's voice is the primary foreground element of the stream.

2. Step-by-Step OBS Studio Sidechain Ducking Setup

Configuring automatic ducking in OBS Studio requires zero third-party plugins and takes under two minutes:

  1. In the OBS Audio Mixer, locate your Desktop Audio or Browser Source Audio track.
  2. Click the gear/three dots icon and select Filters.
  3. Click the + button in the bottom left and add a Compressor filter (name it Auto-Duck).
  4. Under Sidechain/Ducking Source, select your Mic/Aux input.
  5. Apply the recommended broadcast settings from the table below:
Compressor Parameter Recommended Setting Functional Purpose
Ratio 4.0 : 1 to 6.0 : 1 Provides a smooth, natural volume reduction without jarring cutoffs.
Threshold -32 dB to -28 dB Triggers ducking the moment you begin speaking at normal conversational volume.
Attack Time 20 ms to 35 ms Instantly attenuates the background track on the first syllable.
Release Time 450 ms to 600 ms Gradually restores background audio when you stop speaking, preventing awkward audio pumping.
Output Gain 0.0 dB Leaves raw signal unamplified to preserve natural mastering balance.

3. Multi-Track Audio Routing in OBS Studio

Never record microphone audio and desktop audio mixed together onto a single audio track. If a video receives a claim or requires a volume tweak later, single-track audio prevents you from isolating your voice.

Pro Tip: Open Settings → Output → Recording and enable Tracks 1, 2, and 3. Assign Track 1 to Full Mix, Track 2 to Clean Microphone Only, and Track 3 to Desktop Audio Only in the Advanced Audio Properties window.

4. Mastering Levels for YouTube (-14 LUFS Standards)

YouTube normalizes all uploaded videos to approximately -14 LUFS Integrated (Loudness Units Full Scale) with a -1.0 dB True Peak ceiling. If your reaction audio peaks too loudly (+2 dB), YouTube will forcefully depress your overall video volume, making your commentary quiet on mobile phones.
