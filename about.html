About FairReact

FairReact is a revolutionary open platform and dual-video synchronization engine designed to bridge the gap between reaction creators and original artists across the globe.

Our Core Mission

For years, reaction videos on platforms like YouTube have lived in a state of friction: original artists lose millions of rightful views when their full content is re-uploaded, while reaction creators live in fear of copyright strikes, demonetization, and Content ID claims.

FairReact solves this fundamentally through Lossless Master-Clock Dual Synchronization. Rather than re-uploading copyrighted media, FairReact streams the official original creator video in synchronized Picture-in-Picture directly from official servers.

🏆 For Original Artists

Every view, like, comment, and ad impression is attributed directly to the official original video, protecting creator revenue and creative rights.

🎬 For Reaction YouTubers

Creators can react naturally, talk, laugh, and pause without fear of Content ID claims or copyright strikes on their channels.

How The Technology Works

FairReact operates using single-source-of-truth master clock timing. As the reactor plays, pauses, seeks, or adjusts playback rate, our calibration algorithm calculates the exact matching target frame in real-time, delivering a smooth, high-fidelity dual-video cinema experience on both desktop and mobile web browsers.

Zero-Install Universal Access

Whether viewing through our browser extension or our zero-install web player, FairReact is free, lightweight, and accessible to viewers worldwide on Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, and Linux.
