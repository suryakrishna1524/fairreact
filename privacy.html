Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 3, 2026

At FairReact (accessible from https://fairreact-webplayer.vercel.app), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors and creators. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by FairReact and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

FairReact is engineered with a strict privacy-first architecture:

  • No Personal Tracking: We do not require account registration, usernames, passwords, or personal identity documents to use the dual-sync player or generator.
  • Local Processing: Video timeline calculations, frame sync offsets, and PiP coordinates are computed entirely on client devices within local web storage or runtime memory.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, FairReact uses cookies to store user UI preferences (such as audio volume and preferred PiP docking coordinates). These cookies do not contain personally identifiable information.

3. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie & Google AdSense

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our website and other sites on the internet. Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at Google Ad Policies.

4. Third-Party Embedded Content (YouTube)

FairReact utilizes the official YouTube IFrame API to play video streams directly from YouTube servers. YouTube may collect playback metrics in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.

5. GDPR & CCPA Data Protection Rights

We want to make sure you are fully aware of all your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to access, rectify, or erase any personal information. Because FairReact does not store personal profiles on our servers, your browsing session remains anonymous.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at our Contact Us page or via email at support@fairreact.org.
