Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does FairReact protect YouTubers from copyright strikes?

Instead of downloading and re-uploading copyrighted media inside your video edit, you upload your facecam reaction and provide a FairReact link. Viewers stream the official original video live in synchronized PiP, completely eliminating copyright claims.

Do both creators get official YouTube views and watch time?

Yes! Both the reactor video and the original creator's official video are rendered via official YouTube players, registering legitimate views, watch time, and ad impressions for both channels.

Does it work on mobile phones without installing anything?

Absolutely. FairReact's web player works directly in any standard mobile browser (Safari, Chrome, Firefox) with touch controls and picture-in-picture resizing.
