Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How does FairReact protect YouTubers from copyright strikes and Content ID claims?

Instead of downloading and editing copyrighted video files directly into your video timeline (which triggers automated acoustic and video fingerprint matching), you upload only your clean facecam commentary video to YouTube. FairReact streams the official original video live in synchronized Picture-in-Picture directly from the original artist's channel. Because your upload contains 0% third-party video data, it is completely immune to automated claims.

2. Do original creators get official YouTube views, watch time, and ad impressions?

Yes! Both the reactor video and the original creator's official video are rendered via official YouTube embedded player instances. Every viewer watching via FairReact registers as an official view and delivers legitimate watch time and ad revenue to the original artist's YouTube channel.

3. Does FairReact require installing browser extensions or mobile apps?

No. FairReact is a 100% zero-install web application. It runs seamlessly inside any modern desktop or mobile browser (iOS Safari, Android Chrome, Firefox, Edge) with full touch-drag PiP controls and fullscreen support.

4. What is a FairReact Calibration Token?

A calibration token (e.g. v1.NDk6MCwyNzc6MTky) is a compact, URL-safe Base64 string that maps the reaction video's timeline to the original video's timeline. It tells the sync engine exactly when the reactor introduces the video, pauses for commentary, or rewinds.

5. How does the player handle commentary pauses during the reaction?

FairReact features an intelligent Master-Clock State Engine. When the reactor pauses the original video to deliver critical analysis, the child player automatically pauses at the exact frame. When playback resumes, the child player smoothly unpauses with sub-second accuracy.

6. Can I use FairReact for music video reactions?

Yes. Music video reactions are among the biggest beneficiaries of FairReact. Because record labels strictly enforce automated Content ID blocks on sound recordings, streaming the official Vevo music video in synchronized PiP allows music reactors to provide commentary while delivering official streams to the musician.

7. How do viewers access the Dual Sync Player?

Creators include their generated FairReact link in their YouTube video description and pinned comment. Viewers click the link and instantly experience the dual-synchronized stream on their phone, tablet, or desktop.

8. Is FairReact compliant with YouTube's Terms of Service and API Policies?

Yes. FairReact utilizes standard, authorized YouTube IFrame Player APIs. It does not download, rip, or bypass YouTube's content delivery networks, preserving all creator branding and advertising metrics.

9. How do I calibrate a token for my own reaction video?

Simply input the timestamp where the original video begins playing in your reaction stream (e.g. 00:49). FairReact encodes this into a calibration token that you can paste into your video description.
